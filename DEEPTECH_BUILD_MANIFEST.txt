IMPORTANT LIMITATIONS
- These research modules are decision-support primitives and are not wired
  into payment execution or refund authorization.
- Conformal coverage relies on exchangeability and representative calibration
  data; it is not a guarantee of per-merchant conditional coverage.
- Temporal validation requires the caller to supply correct event/availability
  timestamps and an embargo appropriate to each label horizon.
- Permutation importance is predictive reliance, not causal attribution.
- CircuitBreaker and DeadLetterQueue are in-memory, process-local utilities;
  they are not distributed coordination or durable queues.
- The financial digital twin is a simplified scenario simulator, not an
  accounting ledger, audited forecast, or production financial guarantee.
- No production-readiness or provider certification is claimed.
