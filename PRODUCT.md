# MerchantOS — Product context

## Audience
Merchant teams and operators who need to understand customer issues, payment activity, refunds, disputes, evidence, agreements and operational follow-through.

## Product purpose
Bring fragmented commerce operations into one coherent workspace so merchants can investigate with context, make deliberate decisions and preserve an auditable record.

## Core product principles
- AI may interpret information; deterministic code validates critical conditions.
- Consequential actions remain subject to merchant authorization.
- PayPal/provider responses are authoritative for payment state.
- Demo and sample data must be clearly labelled; never present it as live merchant data.
- Do not invent customer logos, certifications, integrations, uptime claims, testimonials, pricing or production readiness.

## Current surface
A React/Vite frontend with merchant workspace routes for overview, customer intelligence, review analysis, transactions, refund investigations, disputes, agreements, milestones, evidence and audit. A public product landing page introduces the controlled pilot and routes visitors to sign-in. Registration is intentionally restricted to the configured single-merchant pilot until the legacy business routes are tenant-isolated.

## Conversion goal
Help a prospective merchant understand the product's operational value, inspect a truthful interface preview, and sign in to the pilot or review the PayPal Sandbox evaluation path.
