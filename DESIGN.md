# MerchantOS visual system

## Direction
Editorial industrial — precise, calm, operational, and unmistakably merchant-focused. The public site should feel like a serious commerce operations product, not a generic AI startup template.

## Palette
- Ink: `#101713`
- Deep green: `#17221A`
- Paper: `#F1F2E9`
- Soft paper: `#E8EADF`
- Signal / action: `#D9F36A`
- Muted text on dark: `#9EAA9A`
- Muted text on light: `#687466`

## Typography
- Display: Manrope, weight 700–800, tight tracking.
- UI/body: DM Sans, weight 400–700.
- Monospace only for code-like workflow preview details.

## Layout and components
- Wide editorial compositions with asymmetric columns and generous vertical rhythm.
- Use borders, rules, typography and negative space for hierarchy; avoid wrapping every element in a card.
- Use the lime accent for action, active state and important signals only.
- Dashboard preview is explicitly labelled illustrative sample data.
- Pricing is qualitative until real commercial terms are approved; do not fabricate prices.

## Motion and accessibility
- Restrained transitions; no perpetual decorative animation.
- Provide visible focus states through native controls and preserve keyboard-operable buttons.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- At tablet/mobile widths, stack content and preserve all primary navigation and workflow information.
